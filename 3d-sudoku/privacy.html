SUDOKU 3D · PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains how Sudoku 3D by Shebo Tech (the "App"), made by Shebo Tech, handles information on iOS and Android.

What we collect

We do not collect any personal information ourselves. The App has no account, no sign-in and no in-app purchases, and it does not use your precise location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Advertising

The App shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's identifiers, approximate location (from your IP address), ad interaction data, and diagnostic data such as crash and performance information. This data is not linked to your identity by us, and the App does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. Google's use of this data is described in its policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Game Center

On iOS you can optionally use Apple Game Center for achievements and best times. This is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy.

Data stored on your device

Your current puzzle, notes and settings are saved only on your device so you can continue later. They are never sent to us, and they are removed when you uninstall the App.

Sharing

We do not sell any data. Apart from the advertising data handled by Google AdMob as described above, nothing is shared.

Retention and deletion

There is no data on our side to delete. Uninstalling the App deletes everything it stored on your device. You can reset your device's advertising identifier in your device settings.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
